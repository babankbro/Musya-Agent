# 02.06 - Plan: Accident Policy Agent

> **Status:** Done
> **Scope:** Specialized sub-agent for the accident domain database (Fact & Data Mart) supporting policy-level documentation and executive dashboards for Area Health 10 (อุบลราชธานี, ศรีสะเกษ, ยโสธร, อำนาจเจริญ, มุกดาหาร).

## Overview
This plan details the implementation of a new "Accident Policy Agent" (or a significant upgrade to the existing RTI Analyst). This sub-agent leverages the newly structured Data Warehouse (Fact tables and pre-aggregated Analytic Marts) to answer strategic, policy-driven questions for executives and health policy makers in Area Health 10. The goal is to translate raw metadata into actionable public health policies, such as targeted helmet campaigns, infrastructure lighting improvements, and strategic checkpoint deployments.

## Architecture Decisions
- **Agent Role & Placement:** We will create a specialized `accident_policy.py` toolset and integrate it into the existing `Policy Brief Pipeline`, specifically augmenting the RTI Policy Analyst (Agent A2).
- **Data Access Strategy:** The agent will use specialized tools that hit the pre-aggregated `mart` tables for macro/strategic queries, and specific `fact` table joins for micro/behavioral queries. 
- **Safety & Scoping:** Queries will be automatically scoped to Area Health 10 provinces (อุบลราชธานี, ศรีสะเกษ, ยโสธร, อำนาจเจริญ, มุกดาหาร).
- **Testing UI:** We will extend the `policy_brief_ui.html` to include a "Policy Dashboard" section specifically for these 7 policy queries.

## 1. Metadata and Policy Application Mapping

The sub-agent will interpret and utilize the database schema across two main levels:

### 1.1 Micro & Behavioral Level (Fact Tables)
*   **`fact_accident_person` (Human Factors):** 
    *   **Fields:** `age`, `sex`, `role_in_event`, `injury_level`, `helmet_used`, `seatbelt_used`.
    *   **Policy Goal:** Prove the correlation between non-helmet usage and youth (< 24 years) fatalities in Area 10 to support "100% Helmet Use in Schools/Organizations" campaigns.
*   **`fact_accident_event` (Environmental Factors):**
    *   **Fields:** `weather_condition`, `road_condition`, `light_condition`, `severity_level`, `latitude`, `longitude`.
    *   **Policy Goal:** Use `light_condition` and `road_condition` combined with coordinates to direct local governments to install lighting or repair roads at high-severity night-time accident spots.

### 1.2 Macro & Strategic Level (Analytic Marts)
*   **`mart_accident_hotspot` & `mart_province_road` (Engineering & Enforcement):**
    *   **Fields:** `hotspot_score`, `road_name`, `dominant_timeband`, `dominant_cause`.
    *   **Policy Goal:** Direct police and local authorities to set up speed cameras or checkpoints on high-risk roads specifically during `dominant_timeband` matching the `dominant_cause` (e.g., drunk driving blockades at night).
*   **`mart_accident_summary` & `mart_province_year` (KPI & Monitoring):**
    *   **Fields:** `year_no`, `month_no`, `death_count`, `top_vehicle`, `top_cause`.
    *   **Policy Goal:** Generate Executive Dashboards to track if monthly policies effectively reduce `death_count` and if motorcycles remain the `top_vehicle`, justifying funding requests.

## 2. Policy Query Questions (The Sub-Agent Tools)

The agent will be equipped with tools to answer these 7 specific policy queries:

**Category 1: Hotspot & Road Infrastructure**
1.  **Top Risk Roads:** "In Ubon Ratchathani and Sisaket, which top 10 roads (`road_name`) have the highest `hotspot_score`, and what is their `dominant_cause`?" (To deliver to highway departments).
2.  **EMS/Police Scheduling:** "For high-severity hotspots, what is the `dominant_timeband`?" (To schedule EMS and police checkpoints).

**Category 2: Human Behavior & Vulnerability** (Requires joining `fact_accident_person` and `fact_accident_event`)
3.  **Helmet Usage by Age:** "What percentage of motorcycle fatalities (`injury_level`='Death', `vehicle_type`='Motorcycle') in Area 10 involved not wearing a helmet (`helmet_used`=FALSE), and which `age` group is most affected?"
4.  **Seatbelt Usage:** "Among severely injured car passengers (`serious_injured`, `role_in_event`='Passenger'), what is the proportion of unfastened seatbelts (`seatbelt_used`=FALSE)?"

**Category 3: Environmental Context**
5.  **Environmental Correlations:** "In Area 10, are high `severity_level` accidents more strongly correlated with `light_condition` (e.g., unlit roads at night) or `road_condition` (e.g., wet/curved roads)?"

**Category 4: Performance & Dashboarding**
6.  **YoY KPI Tracking:** "Compare `death_count` and `serious_injured` between the previous year and the current year in Yasothon, Amnat Charoen, and Mukdahan. Are they decreasing per the national strategy?"
7.  **Festival Shifts:** "Looking at `month_no`, which month has the most dangerous `high_risk_timeband`, and does the `top_cause` change during festival months (April, December)?"

## Risks and Mitigations
| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|--------|------------|
| Complex joins on `fact_accident_person` and `fact_accident_event` might timeout. | High | Use the `geography_id` filter (Area 10 only) early in the CTE or subquery before joining. Ensure indexes on `injury_level`, `helmet_used`, and `event_datetime` are utilized. |
| The LLM may hallucinate the policy correlations. | Medium | Enforce strict JSON output schemas and require the LLM to cite the tool output data exactly in the "key_findings" array. |
